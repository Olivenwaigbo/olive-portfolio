THE QUESTION

What happens when payment systems are treated as intelligence problems?

Payment systems generate enormous amounts of operational data. Yet when transactions fail, remain pending, reverse, or do not reconcile cleanly, teams can still spend significant time piecing together what happened.

This lab explores products that turn those fragmented signals into structured evidence, operational insight and better decision support.

The system establishes the facts first.

PayResolve separates deterministic analysis from AI explanation. This creates a clear boundary between what the system knows and what AI is asked to explain.

05 · PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

How I think about AI in financial operations.

01

Evidence First

The system should establish what happened before AI attempts to explain it.

02

Human in the Loop

AI supports investigation and understanding; operational teams remain responsible for decisions.

03

Traceability

Investigations should be connected back to the transaction and event evidence that produced them.

04

Privacy by Design

The prototype uses synthetic payment data and does not process real customer funds or financial records.

RESPONSIBLE EXPERIMENTATION
