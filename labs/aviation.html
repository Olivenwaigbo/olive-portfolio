OLIVE AVIATION INNOVATION LAB

Exploring the future of aviation through AI & data.

An independent research and prototyping initiative exploring how artificial intelligence, data analytics and digital products can solve complex problems across aviation and aerospace.

WHY THIS LAB EXISTS

Aviation is a complex system. I want to understand it.

I am exploring aviation from the perspective of a Product, Data and AI professional.

The aviation ecosystem brings together aircraft manufacturers, airlines, airports, maintenance organisations, regulators, suppliers and millions of passengers.

The lab exists to research the problems within these systems and prototype practical technology-driven solutions.

RESEARCH AREAS

Four areas I'm exploring.

Aerospace Manufacturing

Production intelligence, quality, supply chain, manufacturing analytics and Industry 4.0.

AI & Intelligent Systems

AI assistants, predictive analytics, anomaly detection and decision-support systems.

Airline & Airport Operations

Flight disruptions, baggage, passenger experience and operational efficiency.

Aviation Data Intelligence

Data analytics, forecasting, segmentation, dashboards and business intelligence.

MY APPROACH

Research first. Prototype second.

I don't want to build technology simply because AI makes it possible.

Each project begins with a problem.

I research the users, understand the operational context, analyse available data, define the product and then build a prototype.

The goal is to demonstrate what could be possible — while being honest about assumptions, limitations and data constraints.
