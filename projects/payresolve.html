01 OVERVIEW

Making payment operations easier to investigate.

Payment transactions move through multiple states and systems. When something goes wrong, the transaction record may not clearly explain what happened.

A transaction can fail, remain pending, reverse after success, experience a provider timeout, or have inconsistent records across payment events.

PayResolve explores how a product can bring these signals together into one investigation workflow.

02 THE PROBLEM
When a payment has an unclear outcome, operations teams need to answer: What happened? What evidence do we have? What should be investigated next?
03 USERS

Designed around the people investigating payment issues.

01

Payment Operations Analyst

02

Product Manager

Uses operational patterns and incident data to identify recurring payment problems and product opportunities.

03

Engineering / Technical Operations

Investigates provider errors, missing events, system inconsistencies and technical payment incidents.

04 PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS

If payment teams can see transaction state, event history, reconciliation results, anomaly signals and evidence-based AI explanations in one workflow, they can investigate exceptions more systematically.

05 HOW PAYRESOLVE WORKS

From transaction data to an investigation.

01

Detect

Transactions and payment events are analyzed for reconciliation exceptions, duplicates and unusual patterns.

02

Reconcile

The reconciliation engine compares transaction states with recorded payment events.

03

Classify

Exceptions are grouped into incident categories and assigned investigation priorities.

04

Explain

The AI layer summarizes the available evidence and proposes an operational next step.

07 PRODUCT DECISIONS

Designing for trust.

01

Evidence First

The system establishes transaction facts before asking AI to explain them.

02

Human in the Loop

AI provides investigation support rather than making autonomous financial decisions.

03

Traceability

Investigation output remains connected to the underlying transaction and event evidence.

04

Privacy by Design

The prototype uses synthetic transactions and does not process real customer payment information.

08 PROTOTYPE DATA

Building against realistic failure scenarios.

To make the prototype useful for testing investigation workflows, PayResolve uses synthetic payment transactions and deliberately injects operational scenarios.

Test scenarios include missing settlements, status conflicts, missing events, provider timeouts, potential duplicate payments and debit-without-settlement cases.

09 AI INVESTIGATION

AI as an explanation layer.

EVIDENCE-BASED AI

The AI does not determine what happened. The evidence does.

PayResolve first assembles structured transaction, event, reconciliation, incident and anomaly evidence. The AI model then uses that evidence to generate an investigation summary and operational next step.

Use available evidence No unsupported facts.
State what is unknown Missing evidence remains unknown.
No autonomous decisions Human review remains required.

11 LIMITATIONS

What this prototype does not claim.

01

PayResolve is not a payment processor and does not move real money.

02

The prototype uses synthetic data rather than real customer payment records.

03

AI-generated explanations are decision-support output and require human review.

04

The anomaly model is an experimental prototype and should not be treated as a production fraud-detection system.

05

The current architecture is designed to demonstrate product and technical concepts rather than production financial infrastructure.
