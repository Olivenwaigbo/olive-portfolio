ABOUT ME

A scientist who learned to build.

OLIVE CHIKA NWAIGBO CPM · M.I.M.
PRODUCT · DATA · AI ✦
01

My career didn't begin in technology. It began in Biochemistry.

I studied Applied Biochemistry because I was fascinated by how things work beneath the surface. Somewhere along the way, that curiosity moved from molecules and experiments to data, systems and technology.

I taught myself data analytics, learned how to turn messy information into useful insights, and eventually found myself working closer to the business and technology teams — translating problems into requirements, dashboards and digital solutions.

Then I discovered Product Management. It brought everything together: research, data, business, technology and people.

Today, I don't just write product documents or talk about ideas. I like to build. I use tools like Python, Streamlit, APIs, AI and data to turn ideas into working prototypes — so teams can see, test and learn before committing significant time and money to development.
